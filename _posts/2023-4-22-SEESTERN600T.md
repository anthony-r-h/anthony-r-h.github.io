# About

The DOXA 300T and 600T share the same basic dive-watch design language, but their cases are quite different. The 300T has a rounded cushion case, while the 600T is more angular and geometric.

![](/files/SEESTERN600T/1/post/c1.png)

I prefer the sharper shape of the 600T case, but wanted to combine it with a dial and handset inspired by other dive watches.

![](/files/SEESTERN600T/1/post/c3.png)

# Build

I started with a Seestern 600T and replaced the original dial, hands, and bracelet.

- Base watch: Seestern 600T
- Dial: DOXA 300T-inspired dial from Yobokies
- Hands: Ploprof-inspired hands from Yobokies
- Bracelet: Strapcode

The 300T-inspired dial works well with the 600T case, and the larger Ploprof-style hands make the watch easier to read.
