# About

The M1W comes with anodized gold accents on each side. I wanted something less flashy, so I made replacement plates and wrapped them in black Alran Sully goatskin.

<img src="/files/M1W_MOD/M1W_mod.jpg">

# Build Notes

I modeled a plate to match the shape of the stock accent, then printed a pair on a Bambu Lab X1 Carbon.

- Lightly sand and clean each print.
- Cut the goatskin with a small allowance around the edge.
- Glue the leather to the face, working from the center outward to avoid trapping air.
- Wrap and trim the edges.
- Refit the plates and check their alignment with the case.

I made the printed plates slightly undersized to account for the thickness of the leather.

STL for the side accent plate: [side_plate.stl](/files/M1W_MOD/side_plate.stl)

It may need a small tolerance adjustment depending on the printer's calibration.
