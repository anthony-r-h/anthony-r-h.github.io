## Alternative Construction



<figure>
  <img src="/files/Boxing Glove/separate-wrist-foam-construction.png" alt="Separate wrist foam being fitted into a glove assembly at a sewing machine">
  <figcaption>Less structured wrist foam, separate from the striking surface padding.</figcaption>
</figure>

The wrist foam is a separate piece from the striking surface padding. That discontinuity decreases stiffness. This construction implies a different padding/liner solution. I think it's probably done to simplify manufacturing, and isn't necessarily a good way to do it.

In this construction, the striking surface liner is glued to the foam, then the whole assembly is stuffed into the glove. So the liner isn't inherently sewn to the edges of the glove.

# Pattern

# Materials

## Outer

1.0–1.2 mm leather.

- Cowhide: common, durable, abrasion resistant.
- Kangaroo: strong for its thickness and weight. Could use thinner leather.
- Horse: very strong, very good abrasion resistance, less flexible  

## Liner

- Heavy polyester tricot
- Goatskin/calf lining leather

# Padding

## Impact surface

### Layers, outside → hand

| Layer | Thickness | Foam | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Contact | 8–10 mm | Soft PU or latex | Soft initially, quick rebound, not mushy |
| Main | 18–22 mm | Medium PU or latex | Takes most of the impact; progressively firmer |
| Support | 6–10 mm | Firm EVA or PU | Spreads load, prevents bottoming out |
| Comfort | 3–4 mm | Soft PU | Separates knuckles from firmer foam |

Target total over knuckles: about 35–45 mm.

### Material choice

- PU: easiest starting point, lots of firmness options.
- Latex: durable, good rebound. Possible main layer.
- EVA: mainly for support.
- Avoid memory foam / slow-recovery foam.

### Foam priorities

1. Firmness (IFD/ILD): force needed to compress it.
2. Compression curve: soft initially, then firmer.
3. Rebound: immediate recovery.
4. Compression set: shouldn't stay crushed after repeated use.
5. Density: useful for comparison, but isn't firmness.

## Wrist (back)

Soft. Not sure how thick yet.

## Wrist (front)

For wrist support. I've seen dense padding and pillow stuffing used here.

## Thumb

## Grip bar

Something for the fingers to close around and keep a consistent fist shape.

- 15–18 mm diameter. Oval, D-shaped or anatomical rather than round.
- Soft foam alone may collapse; a rigid bar may cause pressure points.
- Leaning toward a semi-rigid core with a soft surface. Supportive, with some give when squeezed.
- Options: dense EVA/TPE, solid TPU, TPU lattice, or a light rigid core wrapped in foam.
- TPU lattice looks promising: holds its shape but still compresses.

## Fingers

2–4 mm neoprene at the fingertips.

- Something soft to grip; fills gaps and cushions fingertips/nails against seams.
- Adds friction to help keep fingers curled, without stiffening the pocket.
- ONX mentions dead space as a fit issue. 1v1 users describe neoprene helping them grip and close their fist.
