## About

I wanted to make an everyday belt with a few more refined details in the construction.

I made a simple jig to hold the belt under tension while sanding and painting the edges. Keeping the strap straight made it much easier to get an even finish along the full length.

### Forming the buckle end

I punched the slot for the buckle pin with a 20x5mm oval punch, though I think a 22x5mm punch would work better.

Past the bend line, I split the leather down to half its original thickness. I wet the bend, folded it around the buckle, and clamped it so it would hold its shape. Next time, I might add some padding to give this section more dimension.

### Stitching

I originally planned to use an X-stitch, but it made the construction more complicated than it needed to be. Instead, I punched the holes through the top layer first, glued the folded section in place, and then used those holes to guide the irons through the second layer.

The folded leather was thick enough that I had to open the holes with an awl as I stitched. Otherwise, the thread wanted to twist as I pulled it through.

### Edge finishing

The leather has a dense grain and a wax-coated surface, so I was concerned about the edge paint adhering properly. I sanded the edge fairly aggressively to expose the grain, applied two coats of primer, and followed them with several thick coats of edge paint.

Because the edge was so wide, the paint's surface tension helped it level into a slightly domed finish.

The thick paint caused some trouble when I tried to crease the edge with an FN2 bit. Next time, I want to try cold creasing first and then use as little heat as possible to set the line without disturbing the paint. A different crease tip might also work better.

## Notes

- Space the belt holes about 25mm apart.
- The distance between the holes and the end of the strap depends on the finished belt length and fit, but the spacing should look balanced.
- Point the narrow end of each teardrop hole toward the buckle.
- Revise the pattern.
- 22 stitches in two rows.
