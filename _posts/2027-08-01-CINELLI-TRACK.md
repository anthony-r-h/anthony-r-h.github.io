# Cinelli Gazetta Track

- 2000g
- 1 1/8" headset (28.575 mm)
- BSA 68 mm
- Front QR 100 mm - Rear QR 120 mm

## H+Son Archetype
- 23mm external 17.5mm internal
- ERD 595mm

# Weight budget

| Component           |      Target weight | Notes                                                                                                        |
| ------------------- | -----------------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Wheelset            |    **1250-1450 g** | Biggest opportunity. Carbon tubular if you're comfortable with it, otherwise premium alloy/carbon clinchers. |
| Fork                |      **350-450 g** | Carbon track fork. Saves ~500-600 g over stock. Probably the single best upgrade after wheels.               |
| Stem                |       **90-120 g** | Plenty of options without getting sketchy.                                                                   |
| Handlebar           |      **170-220 g** | Carbon. Track drops are often heavier than road bars.                                                        |
| Seatpost            |      **130-170 g** | Carbon 27.2 if compatible.                                                                                   |
| Saddle              |       **90-140 g** | Berk, Selle Italia SLR C59, etc.                                                                             |
| Front brake caliper |      **110-150 g** | Modern dual pivot.                                                                                           |
| Brake lever         |        **45-70 g** | Single lever only.                                                                                           |
| Tires               | **200-240 g each** | Huge difference between training and race tires.                                                             |
| Tubes               |   **25-40 g each** | TPU saves ~150 g over butyl pair.                                                                            |

# Fork

| Specification | Stock fork                           |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Material      | Columbus chromoly (Cr-Mo) steel      |
| Construction  | Straight-blade steel track fork      |
| Steerer       | Straight 1⅛" threadless              |
| Rake (offset) | **35 mm**                            |
| Axle          | 100 mm QR                            |
| Brake         | Drilled for standard rim brake       |
| Weight        | ~850 g uncut                         |

Straight 1 1/8 (28.575mm) steerer, 35mm rake is unusual.


# Drive train

The SRAM Omnium is approximately 825g complete, including:

- 165 mm crankarms and spindle
- 48T chainring
- Chainring bolts
- GXP bottom bracket, approximately 112 g

It uses a 144 BCD ring, 44.5 mm chainline and the stock chainring is specifically designed for a 1/8-inch chain.

Can change to 3/32" (2 to 2.3 mm teeth) versus 1/8" (3mm teeth).
